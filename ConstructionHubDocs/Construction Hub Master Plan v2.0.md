# Construction Hub Master Plan v2.0

## Vision

Construction Hub is a Commercial Operations and Knowledge Platform built initially for the joinery and cabinetry industry.

The platform exists to:

- Reduce estimating time
- Improve commercial control
- Improve project visibility
- Retain company knowledge
- Improve decision making
- Reduce overhead administration

The goal is not to replace estimators.

The goal is to accelerate workflows, remove repetitive tasks and allow experienced staff to focus on commercial decision making.

Primary Mission:

Turn a week of estimating into approximately one day while maintaining human review and accountability.

---

# Core Principles

1. AI assists, not replaces.
2. Human approval is required for commercial decisions.
3. Knowledge remains with the company.
4. Reliability is more important than automation.
5. Auditability is mandatory.
6. Commercial control takes priority over feature count.
7. Integrate where possible instead of rebuilding mature software.
8. Build Version 1 only.
9. Design architecture for future versions.
10. Avoid technical debt and future rebuilds.

---

# Three Pillars

## Time Saving

Examples:

- AI Takeoffs
- AI Revision Analysis
- AI Handover Generation
- Knowledge Search

## Commercial Control

Examples:

- Quote Management
- Revision Tracking
- Variation Tracking
- Procurement Planning
- Margin Visibility

## Knowledge Retention

Examples:

- Builder Profiles
- Supplier Profiles
- Lessons Learned
- Case Studies
- Company Standards

Knowledge remains with the company even when staff leave.

# Product Positioning

Construction Hub is NOT AI Takeoff Software.

Construction Hub is:

A Commercial Operations and Knowledge Platform.

AI Takeoffs are only one module within the platform.
